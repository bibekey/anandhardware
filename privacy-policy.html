Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information WebTechGhar may collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to protect it.

WebTechGhar ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact WebTechGhar, request a quotation, or submit a project inquiry, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Business or organization name
  • Service requirements
  • Project details
  • Budget or other information you voluntarily provide

We may also receive limited technical information when you visit our website, such as browser type, device type, pages visited and general website usage.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and messages.
  • Discuss website and digital service requirements.
  • Prepare quotations and project proposals.
  • Provide and manage our services.
  • Communicate about ongoing projects.
  • Provide technical support and maintenance.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Protect the website against misuse, spam and security threats.

3. Contact Form

Information submitted through our contact form may include your name, email address, phone number, selected service and project details.

This information is used to respond to your project inquiry and communicate with you about the requested service.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card information, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through the website contact form.

4. Email & WhatsApp Communication

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we may retain information contained in those communications for customer service, project management, quotations and business records.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party communication platforms. Their own privacy policies and terms may apply when you use their services.

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, analytics or improving user experience.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled.

6. Analytics & Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as analytics, hosting, fonts, content delivery networks, payment services or other technical tools to operate and improve our website and services.

These providers may process certain technical or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

Where third-party services are used, we aim to use reputable providers and integrate services that are reasonably necessary for our website or business operations.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide a requested service.
  • Work with service providers supporting our business.
  • Operate website infrastructure and technical services.
  • Comply with applicable laws or legal obligations.
  • Protect our rights, website, users or business.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse or security incidents.

We aim to limit shared information to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no website, online communication method or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to inquiries.
  • Provide services.
  • Manage client projects.
  • Maintain business records.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Meet legal, accounting or administrative requirements.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete or securely dispose of it.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access to, correction of or deletion of information we hold about you.

You may also ask us to stop using your information for certain purposes where applicable.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details provided below.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for general business and professional use.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes that are prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

12. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security or policies of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing them with personal information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website functionality, legal requirements or business practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact WebTechGhar.
